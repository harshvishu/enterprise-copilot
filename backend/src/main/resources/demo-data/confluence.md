# Retail High-Value Alerts: Approved Launch Decisions

Source ID: retail-high-value-alerts
Owner: Retail Banking Product Council
Status: APPROVED
Decision date: 2026-09-15
Scope: Retail high-value transaction alert launch. Not business-account changes or fraud-screening integrations.

This approved decision resolves the earlier outstanding product choices for the retail alert launch.

## Business Decisions

- Only outgoing debit transactions qualify for this launch.
- Incoming credits are excluded from this launch.
- SMS is the launch notification channel; email and push are outside this launch scope.
- When SMS consent is absent, skip the notification and audit the skipped outcome. Do not select another channel.

This page does not establish a customer's actual consent or define enterprise service contracts.
Apply it only within its stated scope. A requested scope exception or conflicting decision still needs human resolution.
